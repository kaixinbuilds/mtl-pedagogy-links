# MTL Pedagogy Links

A links-only guide to research and practice on teaching Chinese, Malay and Tamil in Singapore secondary schools, sorted by exam component. Priority: G3.

One file, no build step. Edit the `D` array in `index.html` to add or change a link.

## Rules for this repo

- **Links only.** Do not copy papers, slides, abstracts or files here. Notes are short descriptions in our own words.
- **OPAL.** Its Terms of Use bar copying or monitoring OPAL by automatic or manual means, and republishing its content, without MOE's written permission. Link to a page with a neutral description. Do not list session titles, presenter names or filenames from OPAL, and do not crawl it.
- **Internal materials stay out.** BVSS teaching materials and past-paper reprints do not belong in this repo.
- **Level labels.** The G3 tag marks sources that name G3, plus Express sources as the closest older-course match.
